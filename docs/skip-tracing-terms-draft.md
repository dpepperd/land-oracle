## Getting this checked cheaply

You do not need a full legal engagement for one short document. Options, roughly from cheapest:
- **SCORE** (score.org): free mentoring for small businesses; mentors often include retired attorneys and can point you to affordable help.
- **Florida Bar Lawyer Referral Service:** typically a low-cost first consultation; ask if a lawyer will do a flat-fee review of one short agreement.
- **Law school clinics** (for example at Florida universities) sometimes help small businesses at no cost.
- **Flat-fee contract review services** (such as UpCounsel, Upwork's legal category, or similar): post this draft and ask for a fixed price to review only.
- **Provider's own guidance:** ask Tracerfy whether they publish a recommended end-user agreement or sample wording for white-label customers; some do.
- **Launch order:** because skip tracing carries the highest legal risk of the planned features, launch it only after a review, even if it comes later than the other features. Mail (postcards) and the existing features carry less risk.
