# What Land Oracle does not do yet

A plain list for anyone showing or selling it. Written from the product as it stands on 2026-11-25. Share it with a broker if they ask. If something here changes, change this page.

## Contact information
- **No owner phone numbers or email addresses.** Land Oracle shows who owns a parcel and their mailing address from the county roll. It does not look up phones or emails. (A lookup feature is being built behind a switch and is off. It needs a data vendor to be chosen first.)
- **No mailing, no texting, no calling from inside the app.** Notes and a lead flag on each parcel are saved, but outreach happens elsewhere.

## Coverage
- **Florida only for customers.** All 67 counties are covered. North Carolina and Texas exist as admin-only previews and are not for sale.
- **County plans limit which counties you can search.** Scout is 1 county, Pro is 3, Statewide is all 67. More counties can be added for an extra monthly fee.

## Data limits
- **Values are the county's assessed (just) values, not market values or asking prices.** They can be far from what a property would sell for.
- **Data comes from the county rolls and is only as current as the latest roll.** It is not live MLS data, and not live listings. A parcel sold last week may still show the previous owner.
- **Absentee owner and other "seller signals" are clues, not proof.** They narrow a list. They do not mean the owner wants to sell, and they can be wrong (for example a second home, or an address typo).
- **Some counties have gaps** in site address, sales history or building details. The app shows blanks rather than guessing.
- **No zoning, permits, liens, mortgages or title information.** Confirm those with the county and a title company before any transaction.
- **Flood zone information may be incomplete in some counties.**

## Tools
- **No saved searches or alerts.** You cannot save a filter and be told when new parcels match.
- **No CRM connection and no team workspace.** One account is one person. Leads and notes are private to that account.
- **CSV export only:** no direct send to a CRM, mailing house or dialer. Scout allows 1,000 rows a month. Pro and Statewide are unlimited.
- **No mobile app.** It runs in the phone's browser, and the map works best on a larger screen.

## The AI Analyst
- **Pro and Statewide plans only**, with a monthly question limit (150 on Pro and 500 on Statewide unless changed).
- It can search, look up one parcel, find nearby recent sales and check flood and highway distance, all from the same data. It does not know about zoning, listings, or anything outside Land Oracle.
- **It can be wrong or slow.** It is told to cite parcels and to remind users to verify with the county. A very broad question can hit a time limit: asking about one county at a time works best.
- It is not legal, tax or appraisal advice.

## Other things to say honestly
- Nobody outside the owner's own team has used it for real work yet. A pilot is the point.
- It is a research tool. It does not replace the county appraiser, a title search, or a survey.
