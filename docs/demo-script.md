# Broker demo script (10 minutes)

For: a broker or team lead (and their agents), shown by Tatjana or the owner. Florida only.
Before you start: sign in with a **real paid-plan account** (not admin) on a laptop with a normal browser window. Pick two or three counties the broker cares about and have a recent deal or listing area in mind. Have one county ready where you know a vacant parcel with an out-of-state owner.

Everything below is based on what the app does today. Anything it does not do is listed in `docs/cant-do-yet.md`: read that page first so you never promise it.

## Minute 0 to 1: the problem (do not touch the screen yet)

Say it in your own words, roughly:

> "Finding who owns a piece of land and whether they might sell means opening the county appraiser's site, one parcel at a time, in every county. It is slow, the sites are all different, and you cannot filter. Land Oracle puts every parcel in one map and lets you filter to the ones worth a call."

Ask one question and listen: **"How do you find off-market land or owners today?"** Their answer tells you which part of the demo to spend the most time on.

## Minute 1 to 3: find land in an area (map and filters)

1. Open the dashboard. Pick a county. Point out the county list is the plan: a plan covers specific counties.
2. Click **Draw search area**, draw a box around a neighbourhood or road the broker knows, and press **Apply search area**. Say: "Only parcels inside my shape."
3. Set **Acreage** (for example 3 to 9 acres or 10 to 20). Show you can click the size again to turn it off.
4. Click a parcel. The popup shows owner, mailing address, acreage, value, and the sales history. Show the link out to the county appraiser's page for that parcel.

What to say: "This is the county's own roll data, in one place."

## Minute 3 to 6: seller signals (the part that is different)

1. Turn on **Absentee owner** (mailing address is out of state, a different zip, or a different house number).
2. Add **Out of state** under Owner type and pick vacant land if the broker works land. Or press the preset **absentee out-of-state vacant land**.
3. Optionally add **No sale shown in N years** to find long-term holders.
4. Show the list: parcel, owner, mailing address, acres, value. Sort or scan for a few that look interesting.
5. Click **Mark as Lead** on one, and add a short note to the parcel ("called, no answer"). Say: "Your own leads and notes stay on your account."

What to say: "These are clues from public records, not proof that anyone wants to sell. It narrows thousands of parcels to a call list."

Be straight about limits if asked: "Market value here is the county's assessed value, not what it would sell for."

## Minute 6 to 8: the AI moment (Pro and Statewide plans)

Only do this if the account is on Pro or Statewide, and check beforehand that the AI Analyst button appears. If it does not, skip this section.

1. Open the **AI Analyst**.
2. Ask something a broker would actually ask, in plain words, for example:
   - "Find 10 to 20 acre vacant parcels in Clay County owned by out-of-state owners, with the biggest ones first."
   - Then: "Show recent nearby sales for the first one."
   - Or: "Is the first parcel in a flood zone, and how close is the nearest highway?"
3. Let it work. It runs real searches on the same data and links every parcel it names. Click one of the parcel links: it highlights on the map.

What to say: "It does the clicking for you, from the same data, and shows its work." Point out its closing reminder to verify with the county before any transaction: it is there on purpose.

If an answer is slow or it says it stopped: "That means it hit its time limit: I would narrow it to one county." Do not try to rescue it live: move on to the next section.

## Minute 8 to 9: export and plans

1. Show **Export CSV** (Scout allows 1,000 rows a month, Pro and Statewide are unlimited). Open the file: it has what the screen shows.
2. Show the pricing page: Scout 1 county, Pro 3 counties and the AI Analyst, Statewide all 67 counties and the AI Analyst. Extra counties are an add-on.

## Minute 9 to 10: the offer

Do not sell hard. Offer a **pilot**:

> "Let us set up two or three of your agents free for 30 days in the counties you work. We only ask that you tell us what is missing."

Then ask for three things:
1. Which counties and which agents.
2. Their one most useful moment, and their one biggest missing piece.
3. A date to check in after two weeks.

Be ready for the honest answer to "do you have owner phone numbers?": "Not yet. This shows who owns it and where their mail goes. Phone numbers are the next thing we are looking at."

## If something breaks during the demo

- Search returns nothing: check the county is one on the plan, and that you pressed **Apply search area** after drawing. Say so out loud and fix it: that is better than hiding it.
- A page errors: refresh once, then move to the next section. Write down what happened.
- Never improvise a claim about data: say "I will check and get back to you."
