## 1. What Land Oracle AI Is (and Is Not)
### What Land Oracle AI Is
Land Oracle AI is a land intelligence and due diligence platform designed to help users understand land faster, with less friction and fewer blind spots.
It consolidates fragmented data—zoning, environmental constraints, access, topography, utilities, and market context—into a single, visual decision surface.
The goal is not more data. The goal is clearer understanding.

### What Land Oracle AI Is Not
Land Oracle AI does not provide investment, legal, or financial advice.
It does not tell you to buy, sell, or avoid a property.
All decisions remain with the user.

### A Note on AI Transparency
AI is used to translate, summarize, and surface complexity.
AI is not used to predict outcomes or replace judgment.

## 2. How to Think About Land with Land Oracle AI
Land Oracle AI helps you understand constraints early.
Land is a constraint problem, not just a pricing problem.
Think in layers, not tables.
Use the platform to guide attention, not certainty.
